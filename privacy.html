Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 July 2026

1. Who we are

Ledgerly ("we", "us", "our") is a personal finance and productivity dashboard operated by Sahil Patel, an individual based in the United Kingdom. For any privacy question or request, contact sahilpatel257@icloud.com.

2. What data we collect

Account data: the email address and password you register with. Passwords are never stored or seen by us in plain text — authentication is handled by Firebase Authentication (Google), which stores only a securely hashed credential.

Content you enter: anything you add to the app — deadlines, tasks, income/expense transactions, notes, workout logs, and investment holdings (ticker, shares, and price you paid). This is the core data the app exists to store on your behalf.

We do not collect: bank account credentials, card numbers, government ID, location data, or advertising identifiers. We do not run analytics or advertising trackers.

3. How we use your data

Solely to operate the app: storing your entries, syncing them across your signed-in devices in real time, and displaying them back to you. We do not sell your data, rent it, or use it for advertising. We do not read your financial data for any purpose other than showing it to you.

4. Third parties we rely on

We use a small number of specialist services to run Ledgerly. None of them receive your name, email, or financial content unless stated:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication & Firestore database) — hosts your account and stores your data securely, access-controlled so only you can read or write it.
  • Yahoo Finance / Finnhub — provide live stock prices. Only the ticker symbol you're tracking is sent (e.g. "AAPL"); no personal or account data is shared with these providers.
  • Frankfurter (European Central Bank rates) — provides currency exchange rates. No personal data is sent; only the currency pair.
  • Anthropic (Claude API) — only if you use the AI Intake feature. Text you choose to paste (e.g. a bank statement or syllabus) is sent to Anthropic for processing into structured entries, and is not used by Anthropic to train their models under their API terms. Don't paste anything into AI Intake you wouldn't want processed by this third party.

5. Where your data is stored

Your data is stored in Google Cloud infrastructure via Firebase, which may process data outside the UK/EEA (including the United States). Google maintains standard contractual safeguards for such transfers. A copy of your data is also cached locally on your device (in browser storage) purely so the app loads instantly — this local copy is cleared when you sign out or clear your browser data.

6. How long we keep your data

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. If you'd like your account and all associated data permanently deleted, email us and we'll action it within 30 days.

7. Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data ("right to be forgotten")
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk)

To exercise any of these, email sahilpatel257@icloud.com.

8. Cookies & local storage

Ledgerly does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It uses browser local storage to keep you signed in and to cache your data for fast, offline-friendly loading. This is functionally necessary for the app to work and isn't used to track you across other websites.

9. Children's privacy

Ledgerly is not directed at, and should not be used by, anyone under the age of 16.

10. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page and, where appropriate, notify you in-app.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: sahilpatel257@icloud.com.
